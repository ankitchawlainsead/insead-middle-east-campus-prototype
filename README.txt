INSEAD MIDDLE EAST CAMPUS — WORKING PROTOTYPE

Open index.html in a browser. Practical Information is a separate page, linked from the campus navigation. No installation or build is required.

Welcome contains the campus introduction, sourced figures, regional stories, programmes, events links, leadership, facilities and other campuses. Practical Information contains contact details, directions, travel costs, accommodation, programme information and accessibility.

Working features: two-page navigation, responsive mobile navigation, image previews, story carousel (buttons, touch and keyboard), programme and story links, expandable sections, campus phone/email links, map and directions, print and INSEAD search.

Design reference: https://www.insead.edu/about-insead/asia-campus
Content and campus photographs: https://www.insead.edu/about-insead/middle-east-campus
Reviewed 15 October 2026. Branding, icons and fonts follow the Asia hub. Campus, story, programme and location images are taken from the existing INSEAD site. Original asset URLs are listed in asset-sources.json.

The events section links to live INSEAD listings; no new event dates have been invented. Visitor costs are marked as indicative. Check current fares and prices with providers. The four figures are the 2007 regional establishment, September 2017 current-campus inauguration, 8 resident faculty, and 90+ faculty who have taught on campus since 2010, as stated on the source page.

Images, fonts, styles and interactions are embedded in the two HTML files. The map, external links, INSEAD search and email/phone applications require an internet connection or the appropriate local application. The search submits to INSEAD; this static prototype does not include a CMS or live programme/event database. Campus navigation and all content are editable in the two HTML files; shared styling and interactions are in style.css and app.js.

This is a review prototype, not a change to the live INSEAD.edu website. Prepared for publication through GitHub Pages.
